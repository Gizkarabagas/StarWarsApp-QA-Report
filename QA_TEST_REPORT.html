Executive Release Verdict: NOT READY FOR PRODUCTION

Two fundamental coding omissions (undeclared Platform imports) cause 100% of user logins to fail and the app to crash immediately upon viewing group notifications. In addition, user credentials are stored in unencrypted plaintext, and StarWarsAPI search is non-functional.

🎤 Your 60-Second Opening Pitch for the Interview (Speak this!)
"Hello team! I've conducted a comprehensive end-to-end QA assessment on StarWarsApp across all five core pillars: Functional, Compatibility, Usability, Performance, and Security.

My bottom-line verdict is that the application is currently NOT ready for release. Out of 41 automated checks and manual code audits, I identified 34 confirmed defects.

The two most critical showstoppers are missing Platform imports: one in LoginScreen that blocks 100% of user logins, and one in GroupHomeScreen that crashes the app immediately when viewing group notifications. Furthermore, passwords are stored in plaintext, SWAPI search is ignored by the server, and the UI breaks when rotated to landscape. I've prepared full reproduction steps and one-line patch remedies in my report."
